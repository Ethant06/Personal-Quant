# Investory — Overview

**Track:** Software  
**Type:** Full-stack web app with AI-powered news analysis and personalization  
**Audience:** College students and beginner investors  
**Repo:** https://github.com/Ethant06/Personal-Quant  
**Product name:** Investory  

## Problem

Beginner investors struggle to stay informed about companies they care about. Useful signal is fragmented across news, filings, macro data, and social media. Financial jargon makes it worse. Students do not have time to read the entire financial press every day.

## Solution

Investory is a personalized AI financial news and education platform.

Users create a watchlist. The product collects relevant company and macro developments, filters noise, and produces a concise daily briefing that explains:

- What happened
- Why it may matter to the company
- How broader economic or political events could affect the business
- What important terms mean
- What remains uncertain
- Where to read the originals

## Non-goals

- Predicting stock prices
- Telling users when to buy or sell
- Acting as a broker or portfolio manager

## Six-week goal

Deploy a web app where a student can create a watchlist, receive a personalized financial briefing, and understand important developments through beginner-friendly, source-linked explanations.
